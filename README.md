# Cat Park

A hillside lawn where eight cats play keep-away with your tennis balls, nap, hide, climb trees and come by for food. There are no levels or rounds. Balls keep dropping from trees and rolling in from the edges, so you can play as long as you like.

## How it plays

- **Catch balls**: touch a loose ball to pop it. Each catch fills the ring at the top, and a ball you knock out of a cat's mouth counts double.
- **Unlock items**: a full ring gives you fish, milk, yarn, a brush or a rare star treat. The tray holds five.
- **Keep the cats awake**: cats ask for things in little bubbles. Leave one waiting and it gets drowsy, then curls up and sleeps. Poke it and it gets up for a few seconds before dozing off again.
- **Give items**: drag an item onto a cat, or tap the item and then the cat. The right one wakes it up and sends it off to play with a burst of energy. The wrong one still helps a little.

## Everything else

- **Click** trees, bushes, the pond, the lamp or the food bowl. Clicking a cat that holds a ball makes it drop it.
- **Drag** props, balls and cats. Double-click a cat to have it follow you, and press Space to call everyone over.
- **Tools** (keys 1-6): hand, treats, feather wand, laser, brush and catnip.
- **Item drawer**: add boxes, cushions and toys, and drop things on the trash spot to remove them.

